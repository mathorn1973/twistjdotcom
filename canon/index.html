The Canon is the committed public ledger of TWIST-J. It preserves exact mathematics, status labels, dependencies, evidence, falsifications, and open obligations.

The current Canon is the head of main in the canonical repository. Version number, tag, content commit, checksum and byte count are stated in STATUS.md there. This page is a reading surface, not a competing authority, and it carries no pins of its own.

Status discipline

Every claim retains its declared label, in the order T-LOCK > T > D > C > H > O > F. The public line carries no T-LOCK: T is the strongest public status, and internal LOCK grade data enters at its evidential grade. No summary is stronger than its committed source. A fired falsifier remains part of the record.

Canon releases use integer numbers only: 1, 2, 3, and so on. The value cff-version in the citation file identifies the Citation File Format schema, never the Canon version.

Axiom: J = 1 + j². Time is a counter. Space is a commutator. Exact arithmetic first. Plenum, not vacuum.
