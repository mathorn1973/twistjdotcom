What follows from multiplying by J?

Theorem [T]. For j = exp(2πi/5) and J = 1 + j², multiplication by J acts on four integer coefficients. In the principal complex embedding it turns by 72° and scales distances by the reciprocal of the golden ratio. The Canon separately assigns the two projections physical readings as electromagnetic phase and gravitational scale [D]. Algebraic identities and projection dictionary.

Open [O]. Choosing a clock requires another step. Do the allowed reading rules force the proposed two-state clock to alternate in one particular way, and is its readout minimal? The Canon calls the proposed alternator β₁. Deriving it would require showing why the other permitted choices are excluded. Clock selection and its acceptance conditions.

When can two integer sites interact reversibly?

Theorem [T]. In the specified model, two sites carry values in ℤ[j], where j is a primitive fifth root of unity. A contact preserves their sum and multiplies their difference by j². The two new values belong to ℤ[j] exactly when the original difference is divisible by 2 in that ring. With all other pairs left fixed, the map is reversible and five applications restore the pair. Contact rule and proof.

Open [O]. The theorem treats a single pair. A separate problem asks whether one reversible update can produce a specified quadratic readout in a finite-field model with a two-state register. It must work on every reachable state, even after the memory is occupied. Its allowed gates and controls must be fixed independently of the desired output. Native contact with memory.

How long does an exact record last?

Finite computation [C]. In six prepared histories of the H3 lattice rule, a fixed trace reader returns 0, +1 or −1 for the three preparations at both stages of cuts 4 through 13. At cut 14, both signed cases have archive account 163, while the reader returns 1/6 and −1/2. The exact signed labels have expired and the reference remains fixed. Preparations, readout and its stopping point.

Open [O]. The physical apparatus question includes how an apparatus prepares and renews its ready state and what counts as the complete post-state record. The finite hold establishes retention for the specified inputs and time interval. Complete apparatus class and record equality.

What would make an algebraic readout a measurement?

Theorem [T] and dictionary [D]. The direct and factored quadratic records agree field by field on all 15,625 checkpoints of the specified finite decoder carrier. Exactly 25 checkpoints carry the zero-support tag. A separate selected instrument adopts an ordered Born law for its declared source and controls. Record factorization · Selected Born law.

Open [O]. A complete physical apparatus must justify what counts as a realized event and a complete post-state record independently of the target outcome, classify the admissible apparatuses, and supply the specified sampling law. The selected Born dictionary fixes a law for its chosen model. Physical apparatus obligation · Event and class conditions.

What has been established about light?

Dictionary [D]. In the selected weighted scalar incidence model, the incidence and evolution readings have the same zero set throughout the registered periodic carrier, including both frequency branches and their common apex. Global cone agreement and its scope.

Open [O]. Can the specified five-component window produce a mathematically massless phase? This needs a complete action, normalization and a checked theorem connecting that action to the claimed limit. Massless-phase obligation.

An earlier route that failed

Falsified [F]. An admitted worldline violates the universal filling bound required by the earlier photon-window proof. That counterexample defeats the compound route at its first necessary condition. The later roughening step was never reached. Filling-bound counterexample · Falsified route.

Does continuity fix the density of dark energy?

Theorem [T]. Within the homogeneous, constant-coefficient model over the rationals, the continuity relation q = 3(1 + w) permits both (q, w) = (0, −1) and (1/5, −14/15). Continuity alone leaves the density exponent q undetermined. Underdetermination theorem.

Open [O]. Can an explicitly specified source and a justified dictionary uniquely select the homogeneous dark-energy density exponent? The selection must follow from the additional structure and cannot assume the proposed coefficient. Density selection obligation.
