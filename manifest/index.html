TWIST-J

Manifest

A. M. Thorn
A finite rule, unbounded becoming.
That is TWIST-J in a nutshell.

One sentence longer: the whole universe, matter, both forces, space and time, flows from a single step, J = 1 + ζ₅², computed entirely in integers, with not a single free parameter.

For the layperson

Picture a coin standing on its edge. Outwardly silent, balanced. Inside, it counts; one step, repeated over and over. Out of that repetition the golden ratio and pi climb out on their own; nothing is plugged in, they simply appear. The space around you is not a stage, and the time you live through is not a river; both are just this counting, read from inside. And what people call empty space is not empty: it is the plenum, a full, counting substance. The universe is not a thing. It is a verb.

For the physicist, for the scientist

TWIST-J has no free dimensional parameter; the single calibration for all of physics is the electron mass, and that only for the sake of units. Gravity and electromagnetism are two projections of one J, the modulus and the argument. The strongest piece is the fine structure constant, derived purely from J; it agrees with the measured value to a tenth of a billionth, it was never aimed there, it simply fell out of the computation. The proton to electron mass ratio, 6π⁵ with a small correction, differs from measurement by one part in a million, and that remainder is admitted, not celebrated. Dark energy carries a fixed prediction, w = −14/15, now standing trial before the DESI data. And the discipline is falsification: the exponential metric was refuted by the ringdown of gravitational wave GW250114, and out of that refutation grew, the same day, a closed gravitational wave sector; the one derivation still owed, O-TT-GAUGE-DERIVATION, stands with its falsifier armed.

For the mathematician

The foundation of TWIST-J is the cyclotomic field Q(ζ₅). Multiplication by the generator J is a four by four integer matrix with entries only −1, 0, 1, determinant one, trace three. The step group is the binary icosahedral 2I = SL(2,5), and through the McKay correspondence it leads to the affine Dynkin diagram &Etilde;₈. The classification of stable classes gives exactly 313 attractors (125 + 125 + 62 + 1). Every claim carries an explicit status, definition, axiom, identity, theorem, computation, open wall, and no summary is stronger than its own label.

For the programmer, for the engineer

In practice TWIST-J is a machine of four additions per step and zero multiplications; no drift, just exact integer arithmetic, verified bit by bit on two different architectures, with a SHA-256 hash over every result. A by-product, JAM, is a small language model built to test whether the J-algebra helps as a computational layer. Almost everything failed; a linear transform placed before a learned layer is absorbed into it without remainder. Two things survived, the DJ motor and the BinDJ positional encoding. Inference runs in INT4.

For the philosopher

The axiom reads: reality is the closed integer J-plenum, with no outer boundary, no outer clock, only an inner reading. The total energy sums to zero, not as a wish but as a theorem. Time is a counter, space is a commutator, curvature is what remains. The central principle: if it cannot be calculated in integers, it is not physics. It is at once a claim about the nature of the world and about the method of knowing it; a map of responsibility, not a request for faith.
